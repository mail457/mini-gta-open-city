# MINI GTA — OPEN CITY · Controls

## Keyboard & mouse

| Input | On foot | In a vehicle |
|---|---|---|
| `W` | Walk forward | Accelerate |
| `S` | Walk back | Brake, then reverse |
| `A` / `D` | Strafe left / right | Steer left / right |
| `Shift` (hold) | Sprint | — |
| `Space` | Jump | Handbrake (slide / quick stop) |
| `E` (or `F`) | Enter the nearest vehicle (within ~4 m) | Exit — you step out on the driver's side |
| `Mouse` | Orbit the camera around the player | Orbit the camera (it auto-trails the car when you stop moving the mouse) |
| `Esc` / `P` | Pause | Pause |
| `R` | Restart the delivery run | Restart the delivery run |
| `M` | Mute / unmute | Mute / unmute |
| `F3` | Toggle the performance overlay | Same |

## Mouse capture

Click anywhere on the 3D view to capture the mouse (pointer lock) so camera movement
feels like a proper third-person game. Press `Esc` to release it — the game pauses while
the mouse is free. Click **RESUME** to continue.

If your browser blocks pointer lock (some privacy settings do), the game is still fully
playable: use `A`/`D` steering and the auto-follow camera.

## Menus

- **Start screen:** `PLAY` starts the game, `HOW TO PLAY` shows the controls legend.
- **Pause screen:** `RESUME`, `RESTART RUN`, `CONTROLS`, `MUTE: ON/OFF`, `QUIT TO MENU`.
- **Mission result:** `CONTINUE` (free roam) or `RESTART` (run it again). `Enter` = continue, `R` = restart.

## Tips

- Parked cars sit along the start street and in the parking lot; traffic cars can also be jacked — they slow down as you grab them.
- The compass at the top and the gold diamond on the minimap always point to your current objective.
- The van is slower but sticks to the road; the sport car is fast and happy to slide — use the handbrake into corners.
