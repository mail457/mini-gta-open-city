# Manual acceptance checklist (Mac, Safari & Chrome)

Use this for a final human pass on the published URL (takes ~5 minutes).

| # | Step | Expected result | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the HTTPS URL | Start screen with the big **MINI GTA / OPEN CITY** title, a lit city behind it | ☐ |
| 2 | Press **PLAY** | Start screen disappears; HUD appears: money `$0` top-right, mission panel top-left, minimap bottom-left, compass top-centre | ☐ |
| 3 | Click the game view | Mouse cursor hides (pointer lock); moving the mouse orbits the camera | ☐ |
| 4 | Hold **W** | The character walks forward with animated legs; the camera follows | ☐ |
| 5 | Hold **Shift + W** | Faster run + wider field of view | ☐ |
| 6 | Press **Space** | Character jumps and lands on the pavement | ☐ |
| 7 | Walk into the **gold marker** | Objective changes to “Get into any vehicle” | ☐ |
| 8 | Walk to the parked **sedan** nearby, press **E** | You are inside the car; speedometer appears; camera pulls back | ☐ |
| 9 | Hold **W** | Car accelerates smoothly; wheels spin; speed in km/h climbs | ☐ |
| 10 | **A / D** while driving | Car steers; body rolls slightly into the turn | ☐ |
| 11 | **Space** while driving | Handbrake: the car slides/brakes hard | ☐ |
| 12 | Follow the compass/minimap to the drop-off | Timer counts down in the mission panel (turns red under 10 s) | ☐ |
| 13 | Stop inside the gold drop-off ring | **MISSION COMPLETE** overlay with reward `+$___` and time; money increases | ☐ |
| 14 | Press **Enter** (or CONTINUE) | Back to free roam; toast “FREE ROAM” | ☐ |
| 15 | Press **Esc** | Pause screen; the world freezes | ☐ |
| 16 | Press **Esc** again / RESUME | Game resumes | ☐ |
| 17 | Press **R** | Run restarts at the start street, money kept | ☐ |
| 18 | **Different car:** jack a moving car (stand near a road, press E) | You take over the car | ☐ |
| 19 | Console (Safari: Develop → Show JavaScript Console) | No red errors | ☐ |
| 20 | Safari “WebGL not supported” hint | Should NOT appear | ☐ |

**Good looks like:** 30–60 FPS on an Apple-silicon Mac in Chrome/Safari, smooth camera, cars stopping
before kerbs rather than passing through them, pedestrians on the pavement, the minimap tracking you.

**Reporting a bug:** note the step number, what you saw, the browser, and a screenshot of the console.
